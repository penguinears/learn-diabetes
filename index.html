CARB COUNTING 1

What are carbs? 🍞

Carbohydrates are found in lots of foods we eat every day. When we eat carbohydrates, our body breaks down many of them into glucose.

If you have Type 1 diabetes, knowing how much carbohydrate is in your food can be an important part of managing your blood glucose.

First, let's practise spotting foods that contain carbohydrate.
