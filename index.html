CARB COUNTING 1

Getting to know carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are found in lots of the foods we eat. When we eat carbohydrates, our body breaks many of them down into glucose.

If you have Type 1 diabetes, knowing how much carbohydrate is in your food is an important part of understanding how food can affect your blood glucose.

Foods with lots of carbohydrate Bread, cereal, rice, pasta, potatoes, sweets and many other foods contain significant amounts of carbohydrate.
Foods with little carbohydrate Foods such as eggs, meat, fish, cheese and oils usually contain very little carbohydrate.

But there is a catch: you cannot always tell exactly how much carbohydrate a food contains just by looking at it. Later, we'll use scales and food labels to find out.
